About The Event

Where Governance Meets Innovation

A landmark summit shaping the future of cybersecurity and GRC leadership in Asia.

GRC Asia Conclave – India is the most prestigious gathering of Governance, Risk & Compliance professionals in the Asia-Pacific region, brought to you by Ampcus Cyber.

Held in the financial capital of India, Mumbai, on 27 June 2026, this one-day power-packed summit unites Chief Information Security Officers, Risk Officers, Compliance Leaders, and Technology Visionaries for an unparalleled exchange of ideas, strategies, and solutions.

From boardroom-level policy discussions to hands-on threat intelligence briefings — the Conclave is where strategic decisions are made and industry alliances are forged.
